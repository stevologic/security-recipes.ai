Adopt secure AI at the pace your organization can govern.

Begin with a small, reviewable loop. Expand access only when the workflow produces consistent evidence and named owners can support it.

Small teams

Start with one repository.

Use one finding class, follow the AI agent setup guides for the tool you already trust, keep existing tests, and require review for every change.

Enterprise teams

Scale through governed lanes.
